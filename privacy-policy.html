Introduction

Fun Learning Adventure ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users of our App.

Local Device Information

The App operates entirely on your device and does not transmit any data to external servers. Any information used by the App (such as text-to-speech preferences) is stored locally on your device only.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not:

  • Collect personal information from children under 13
  • Share any user data with third parties
  • Use analytics services that collect personal information
  • Display third-party advertisements
  • Require user accounts or registration

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no information to use or share.

Third-Party Services

The App may load external resources (such as CSS and JavaScript libraries) from content delivery networks (CDNs) for functionality purposes. These resources are loaded directly by your device and we do not receive any data from these requests.

Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breach. All user preferences and settings are stored locally on your device.

Your Rights

As we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. You can uninstall the App at any time, which will remove all local data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
